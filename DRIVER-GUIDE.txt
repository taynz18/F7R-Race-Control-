F7R RACE FLAGS

GET STARTED
Extract the whole ZIP, then open RaceFlags.exe. Keep the _internal folder beside it.
The app connects to race control automatically.

POSITION AND SIZE
Drag the centre of the flag to move it.
Drag any edge or corner to resize it.
The Size slider scales the flag while keeping its current proportions.
Your position, width, height and transparent-mode preference are remembered.

TRANSPARENT MODE
When enabled, the overlay disappears after race control clears the flag.
Switch it off to show a positioning panel while no flag is active.
Active flags and connection warnings still appear.

CLOSE
The X on the flag and the Close Race Flags button both exit the entire local app.
Minimise the controls window if you want to keep receiving flags while driving.

Use a windowed or borderless-windowed game. Exclusive fullscreen may cover desktop overlays.
An OFFLINE panel means the current race signal cannot be confirmed.

The Racing Sans One typeface is bundled under the SIL Open Font License.
Its license and font information are included in _internal/assets.

LIVE MESSAGES AND FCY COUNTDOWN
Race control can show a short message below any active flag.
During a timed FCY, the remaining time appears beside FCY. All drivers use
one server deadline. FCY reaches FCY ACTIVE and stays yellow. A separate PREPARE TO RACE
countdown is half yellow/half green and becomes full GREEN when it expires.
If the connection is lost, the app shows OFFLINE; it never assumes GREEN.
At 00:00 it waits for the confirmed server signal, so a later RED always wins.
The current Windows download is required to see messages and countdowns.

COUNTDOWN PHASES
FCY 00:20 -> FCY ACTIVE (yellow stays active).
PREPARE TO RACE 00:10 (half yellow / half green) -> plain GREEN.
Keep this app updated so it recognizes the new Prepare to race signal.
Plain colour flags intentionally show their colour without a colour-name label.

CHECK YOU OPENED THE UPDATED APP
The driver controls title bar now shows the exact version (for example v0.1.37).
Close all older copies before testing. Extract the complete new ZIP to a new
folder and launch RaceFlags.exe from that folder, rather than an old shortcut.
An older app that still prints BLUE or RED does not support Prepare to race.

RADIO AUDIO
Use Radio audio to mute/unmute. The volume slider controls this app's calls.
Test audio plays the green-flag call. Your volume and mute choices are saved.
A buzz/mic click precedes your league's voice calls when the flag changes.
FCY and Prepare to race use the shared timer for the final countdown numbers.
A newer flag interrupts older audio; muted calls are not queued for later.
